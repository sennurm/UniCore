## Application Context (maintained by the SME clarifier)

### Field / Domain
University management system for a large Indian multi-campus university. Modules: authentication/authorization (own identity store, password + OTP), student onboarding (import-only from existing ERP), central timetabling (labs, electives, combined classes), QR-based attendance with anti-fraud controls, faculty task management (hierarchical downward assignment; tasks carry School-scoped shared **tags** — free-form, assigner+assignee editable, School Incharge-curated with entity-semantic rename/merge/deactivate, inherited by bulk copies and recurrence cycles, no personal data in tag names), per-School configurable student promotion, syllabus coverage tracking, question paper generation (bank + blueprint, AI-assisted, sealed until Exam Cell release), and a two-tier PA (email+tasks) client — draft-only AI for ~20 leadership roles, AI-free mail+tasks for the staff tier. Admissions, fees, and exam conduct/valuation are out of scope (external ERP/exam systems; answer-script evaluation confirmed out of scope 25-07-2026 — subject-mapped evaluator eligibility recorded for a possible future module).

### Users & Roles
Students; teaching staff in five grades (Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, Tutor, Assistant Teaching Staff — all "Faculty Member" tier; Tutors/ATS cannot author question-bank entries); HoDs; **School Incharges** (one per School — hold every School-scoped power: promotion config+ratification, calendar approval, kind taxonomy, tag curation, attendance-impact policy, School Admin designation); **Faculty Deans** (one per Faculty Division — FET, FMC, FHSS, FSC, FMS, FHS, Agri); **Dean Academic Affairs** (University-level, line manager of all Faculty Deans); Admin/office staff; Non-teaching support (Exam Cell led by the **Controller of Examination**, Timetable Cell — still the central timetable author, lab assistants); the full non-academic staff tree (Dean Research, Dean Student Welfare & Chief Proctor, Dean IQAC, Finance Officer, Dean Admin & R&A, PRO chains — accounts with minimal access: leave, tasks, tier-2 PA; **Alumni get no accounts**); Chancellor (top-of-hierarchy singleton, minimal account approving VC/Registrar leave). **No Pro-Chancellor exists** (removed 25-07-2026 per the university org chart). Org/access source of truth: `requirements/sources/module_access_matrix.xlsx` (25-07-2026). **PA (email+tasks) is two-tier:** tier 1 "Access to AI" (~20 leadership roles) gets the full EML client with draft-only AI; tier 2 (HoDs, all teaching staff, most admin staff — ~2,000 users) gets email client + tasks with **no AI features**; students/security/canteen/alumni get no PA. **Reporting chain** (AUTH-configured, drives leave routing and task escalation): Class In-charge → HoD → School Incharge → Faculty Dean → Dean Academic Affairs → VC → Chancellor; Registrar → Chancellor; Principal/Director → VC (Principals are absent from the org chart — existence flagged as an open question). Non-academic staff resolve to a **unit head** from the AUTH role registry (e.g., Exam Cell → Registrar); the registry also names who designates each auxiliary role (School Admin ← School Incharge, subject coordinator ← HoD, School exam coordinator ← Controller of Examination, HR designate ← Registrar, VC/Pro-VC office list ← Super Admin). Leave/escalation routing **cascades past levels that are on approved leave or vacant** (cause recorded per hop; Chancellor terminal; chronic vacancy alerts after a configurable 5 working days). Leave module (10-leave-management.md): single-approver routing with auto-cascade past approvers on leave and standing upward delegation; student leave types have School-configurable attendance impact (counts-as-present / absent / condonation-evidence); staff have simple per-type balances (accrual/LOP stays in external HR). Faculty leave/absence triggers **rebalancing**: ranked clash-free substitute suggestions to the HoD (never auto-assigned); faculty can also **swap** specific class occurrences by mutual consent (HoD notified, not a gate; occurrence-level only, one hop, no permanent swaps). **No parent/guardian or external-examiner access.** Authorization is RBAC with org-unit scoping over the hierarchy University → Faculty Division → School → Department → Program → Section. Terminology: "Faculty Division" = academic grouping; "Faculty Member" = teacher; "Class In-charge" = section owner (sole authority to correct captured attendance, with mandatory reason). Users hold multiple roles (an HoD may also be a Class In-charge), and leadership roles support **additional charge** — one person holding the same role at multiple org units (the org chart itself shows Faculty Division FMC held by Dean FET and FHS by Dean FMS) — under a strict **singleton rule**: at most one active HoD per Department / School Incharge per School / Faculty Dean per Faculty Division / Class In-charge per Section, changed only via atomic supersede (revoke + issue together). **Term-scoped assignments:** Class In-charge grants and faculty subject allocations (carried by the term's published timetable) are valid only for the current semester/year per the School; promotion ratification publishes a per-Section term-closure event that revokes these grants and archives the timetable; every new term requires fresh designation — nothing rolls over. **Sections are per-term instances** (Program × term × label, labels may repeat across terms) created by the Timetable Cell during term setup; org-unit CRUD above Section (Faculty Division/School/Department/Program) is Super Admin-owned, deactivate-never-delete. Each School uploads a per-term **academic calendar** (start/end, exam-date ranges, special events; School office uploads, School Incharge approves, versioned) which also carries the term-archival backstop date; campus holidays stay with System Admin; exam/special-event dates drive soft warnings only. **Attendance freeze:** triggering a Program's promotion run freezes its attendance corrections; post-freeze corrections commit only via an open dispute (non-ratified students), retro counts-as-present leave marking stays exempt until ratification, and never-opened Periods resolve post-freeze by HoD write-off only.

### Regions
India, multiple campuses, 15,000+ students. All personal data resides in India-region infrastructure. IST timezone, DD-MM-YYYY dates, English UI (MVP). Programs are semester-based or year-based — each School decides, and each School owns its own promotion workflow.

### Legal & Regulatory Constraints
- **DPDP Act 2023** — notice + versioned consent; separate explicit consent for attendance geolocation (only pass/fail proximity retained, never location traces); correction/erasure grievance flow (academic records carry statutory retention exemptions); breach notification duties.
- **No minors:** stakeholder decision (24-07-2026) — all students are 18+ at admission; the system carries **no** parental-consent machinery and **no** DOB age guard (DOB is imported but never age-checked). Residual risk accepted: if admission policy ever admits a minor, UniCore has no consent safeguard or signal.
- **UGC/AICTE norms** — minimum-attendance norms (commonly 75%) gate promotion/exam eligibility; thresholds must be School-configurable, never hardcoded.
- **No biometric data anywhere** — QR attendance was chosen partly to avoid it.
- India SMS/DLT regulations apply to OTP delivery.
